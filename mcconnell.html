    How to play

    Search any active NBA player to make a guess. Each guess shows how it compares with the mystery player: team, conference, division, position, height, age and jersey number.

    Height, age and number turn yellow when they’re within 2 of the answer, with an arrow pointing toward it. Positions come from ESPN (G, F, C); hybrids like G-F and F-C are derived from our roster data and height, and share a yellow match with either side.

    Daily mode has one mystery player per calendar day, the same for everyone. Unlimited lets you keep going.
