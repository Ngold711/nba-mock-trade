Season awards

AwardScore = a Game Score–style production blend (points, shooting efficiency, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers) × team win % × availability. Projected games = games played ÷ team games × 82; players under 65 carry a “65-game risk” badge and are left off MVP, DPOY, Sixth Man, Most Improved and All-NBA ladders (All-Star and Rookie of the Year are exempt here). Games started come from ESPN box scores; rookies are inferred (no 2025–26 stats, age 24 or under). ESPN doesn’t publish All-Star selection counts, so career honors from ESPN are shown instead. A statistical model, not a prediction of votes. Not affiliated with the NBA or ESPN.
